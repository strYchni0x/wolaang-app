countries.bin: country borders from Natural Earth, 1:10m Admin 0 - Countries
(ne_10m_admin_0_countries, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). Public domain.
Converted to a compact binary format (names in German, coordinates rounded to 1e-4 degrees).

Correction: the German-Swiss border at Konstanz (Natural Earth gives Konstanz south of the
Seerhein to Switzerland) is taken from OpenStreetMap, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
